Chimora is online only. No sign-in is needed: on first launch the game creates an anonymous account on our own server (Germany) and the game starts right away. There is no email, no third-party login and no chat.

To test online features: Menu > Friends shows your friend code. A second device or a second install can add it. Menu > Account shows the transfer code, the online rules, the privacy note and "Delete account..." (deletes the account and all data on the server at once, guideline 5.1.1(v)).

In-app purchases (the game is free): Menu > Shop. The first time, the shop asks for the player's age (the shop is closed under 13; enter an adult age to test). Every offer lists its exact contents, its effect and the price; nothing is random. To test a purchase: open a tab, choose an offer, choose the price and confirm with the sandbox account. "My purchases" lists every purchase, "Restore purchases" brings back unlocks and the Wayfarer Pass (guideline 3.1.1), "Spending limit" shows the monthly limit, "Help with purchases" opens the help page.

Uses only standard HTTPS (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO). No tracking, no analytics, no ads.

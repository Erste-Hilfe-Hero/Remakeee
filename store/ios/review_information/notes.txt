What the app is: Chimora is a turn-based monster-taming RPG in pixel art for players aged 9 and up (mild fantasy violence: creatures faint, no blood). It is a single-player story game with optional online features with friends. Chimora is online only.

How to start: no sign-in is needed. On first launch the game creates an anonymous account on our own server (EU) and the game starts right away: choose a language, tick the box to accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (both open from links on the same screen), then New Game. There is no email, no third-party login and no chat.

To test online features: Menu > Friends shows your friend code. A second device or a second install can add it; friends exchange set greetings, Lantern Mail, gifts and echo duels against each other's team. Menu > Account shows the transfer code, the online rules, the privacy note and "Delete account..." (deletes the account and all data on the server at once, guideline 5.1.1(v)). "Enter a code..." there and "I have a code" on the title screen only take a transfer code to move your own account to a new phone; they unlock no content. The app has no promo or redeem codes.

In-app purchases (the game is free): Menu > Shop (end of the menu list; next to Satchel once the first Rift is sealed). On first open the shop asks for the age (closed under 13; enter an adult age to test). Tabs: Featured, Advantages, Packs, Pass, Looks, Riftglass, More. Every offer lists its exact contents, effect and price; nothing is random. Riftglass prices show the euro value. Monthly Card (consumable, 4.99 EUR): 300 Riftglass at once plus 100 a day for 30 days, collected in the shop; not a subscription, never renews. Champions (non-consumable): one fixed Spirling at ring 50, once per account. Bundles and daily and weekly offers have fixed contents; their end is shown as a fixed time, never a countdown. Wayfarer Pass (auto-renewable subscription, 4.99 EUR a month) is the premium row of the 30-tier pass in Shop > Pass; its page lists the benefits, the price, the automatic renewal and how to cancel. The free row needs no purchase. To test: pick an offer and its price, confirm with the sandbox account. Shop > More: My purchases, Restore purchases (unlocks and the pass, guideline 3.1.1), Spending limit, Help with purchases. Once after the first act, adult accounts see one Starter Pack note (Show offer / Not now), never in battle.

Riftglass in play (new in 1.3.2): Riftglass the player owns can be spent at fixed prices: continue a lost story battle, heal or Growth x2 (Menu > Riftglass), a bond boost, bringing a trip home early, relighting the Lamp Watch lamp, retrying a Warden. With too little Riftglass nothing opens: no purchase is offered in battle, after a defeat or while bonding. A gift of 20 Riftglass arrives once a day when online. Hidden for accounts under 13.

External services: only our own server (Contabo GmbH, Munich, Germany, processor under Art. 28 GDPR; server location: data centre Lauterbourg, France, EU) for game data, saves and friends, and Apple: StoreKit for purchases and the App Store lookup to see if an update is out. Reminders are local notifications on the phone, no push service. Uses only standard HTTPS (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO). An anonymous progress count (how far players get) stays on our own server. No tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs.

New in 1.3.0: a daily offline goal in the Brindlewick lighthouse (Lamp Watch, reached a few minutes into the story): light the lamp once per day, a lost Spirling visits and joins through the same bonding minigame as in battle (Mine! button by default). The daily light is free.

Regional differences: none. Same content everywhere; only the language (14) and local store prices differ.

Regulated content: none. No gambling, no loot boxes or paid random items, no real-money prizes, no user-generated content besides the Wayfarer name (filtered, can be reported), no free chat, no health, medical or financial features.

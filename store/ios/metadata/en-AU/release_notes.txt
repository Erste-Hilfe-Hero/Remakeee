Chimora 1.3.0

NEW: THE LAMP WATCH
- Light the lamp in the Brindlewick lighthouse once a day. Each night a lost Spirling follows the light. Answer it with your Echo call and it joins you.
- On night 7 a rare Gleam Spirling comes, and you get a new keepsake: the Lamp Lens.

EASIER TO READ
- Talk and battle text is larger, centred and sits above the buttons.
- No more Spirling trailing behind you in towns.
- Before you first play, you accept the Terms of Use in one short step.

AN EASIER START
- Choose your difficulty when you begin a new journey.
- A shorter prologue gets you to your first Spirling sooner.
- Act I is gentler while you learn the ropes.

BATTLE
- The Auto button now sits at the side of the battle screen. Wild battles only, or every story battle. Wardens and rivals ask first.

FINDING YOUR WAY
- The goal line and the minimap are free in Act I.

LOOK AND COMFORT
- Day and night, weather and the Retro filter are now free. Turn them on or off whenever you like.
- Optional notifications: a short note when a roaming trip is over or a new Find of the Day is waiting. Off unless you turn them on.
- Many UI fixes in all 14 languages.

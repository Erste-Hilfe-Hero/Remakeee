Chimora 1.3.2

- A brand-new shop: Riftglass packs, a Monthly Card, Champion Spirlings, bundles, and daily and weekly offers. Every offer shows exactly what is inside, nothing is random.
- Riftglass now works in the game too: continue after a defeat, heal your team, boost growth or a bond, skip the wait of a trip, face a Warden again right away and more.
- A daily gift of 20 Riftglass when online.
- A new pass with 30 tiers and a free row for everyone.
- Gleam forms now come in 5 kinds, from the classic Gleam to the very rare Opaline Gleam.
- New weather: rain, fog, wind, storms and snow change the world and some battles. It is on from the start and can be adjusted in the settings.
- 35 riddle stones and puzzle boards across all regions.
- Many hidden secrets to find just by playing.
- Maps connect better: no more dead ends, and new houses to visit in town.
- Better fit on large screens and tablets.
- Many bug fixes.

Chimora is a turn-based role-playing game in retro pixel style. As a Wayfarer you set out from a lighthouse on the coast of Veydal, choose your first partner and follow Mirela's map book across the region. Somewhere out there Rifts are tearing open, and only you can seal them again.

TRUST, NOT CAPTURE
Wild Spirlings are never caught. You win their trust with the echo call: the Spirling calls a sequence, you answer it. Listen well and you gain a friend.

TACTICAL BATTLES
• Eight elements, each strong against two and weak against two
• Energy instead of move points, traits, held items and battle fields
• Battles with several Spirlings per side, tag battles and Wardens with several phases
• Damage preview, battle log, auto battle and double speed
• Four difficulty modes, from Story to Rift-Born with level caps

A WORLD THAT CHANGES
Explore villages, cliffs, marshes and caves, find hidden paths and gear that opens new places. Day and night, festivals and weekly challenges keep Veydal alive.

TOGETHER ONLINE
• Add friends by code, send greetings and Lantern Mail
• Echo duels against your friends' teams
• A shared world goal every week and a weekly board
• Your save is kept safe on our server in Germany and moves with you to a new phone

FAIR
Buy once, play everything. No in-app purchases, no ads, no loot boxes, no energy timers. No tracking.

Note: Chimora needs an internet connection.

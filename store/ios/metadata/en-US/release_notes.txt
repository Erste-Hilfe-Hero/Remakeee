Chimora 1.2.0

ACT IV: SOLENNE
- A sun-bleached new act: Ochrewell, the Glasswind Flats, a Caravanserai ladder and the Mirage Kiln.
- Three new Rifts and their Wardens. All twelve seals now stand between you and the finale.

AFTER THE FINALE
- Vellmarch: a whole post-game region in the Era II look. 5 towns, 6 roads, 4 Rifts and a story with a betrayal in every chapter.
- Rematches against all 12 Wardens. They have had time to think about losing.
- Echo Tower with 100 floors, Master Rifts, the Random Rift, a weekly giant, a final exam and New Game+.
- Spirlings keep growing past ring 60. Keep going.

STORY AND TRUST
- Two clans, eleven strangers and a Ledger in the pause menu that remembers whom you trusted. A favour can pay off. Or get you robbed.
- Lore quests for six new legendaries, each with a guide who may not be what they seem.
- Side stories in every act and a strangers' camp at the edge of the Wide Rift.

SPIRLINGS
- Over 200 new Spirlings: now 70 of their own in every act.
- Gleam forms in the wild, with a chain bonus and a Spirlog counter.
- Later stages now look like their earlier selves, grown up.
- Your lead Spirling walks behind you. It remembers how you treat it.
- The Ink Oath, hidden gifts from a black-market informant, and offspring that inherit the family business.

NEW PLACES
- The Burrow: a hollow you can claim. The squatter disagrees.
- The Wake: a daily funeral show in Gravers' Hall.
- Hollowgate Reserve: a wildlife park run by smugglers.
- Cartographer's stones, landmarks and map progress per region.

ONLINE (needs an internet connection)
- Live duels with friends, leagues with seasons, raids with a weekly guardian.
- Bunds with donations and weekly goals, trading, wish lists, gifts and timed events.

LOOK, SOUND AND COMFORT
- Every act has its own look, windows light up at night, and the lighthouse finally looks like one.
- Era music for regions, dungeons, battles and Wardens.
- Battle screen: HP bars per era, coloured type badges, matchup hints and a colour-blind mode.
- A gentler first 15 minutes. News now appear under "What's new" on the title screen.
- New optional shop offers.
- Many more texts translated in all 14 languages.
- Plenty of fixes: saves, settings, restoring purchases. Turning the phone clock back no longer pays out daily rewards twice. Nice try.

Chimora 1.3.2

- A rebuilt shop with clearer texts: every pack and offer says in plain words what is inside and what it does. Nothing is random.
- 70 new Spirlings, 10 in every region.
- Gleam forms now come in 5 kinds, from the classic Gleam to the very rare Opaline Gleam.
- New weather: rain, fog, wind, storms and snow change the world and some battles. It is on from the start and can be adjusted in the settings.
- 35 riddle stones and puzzle boards across all regions.
- Many hidden secrets to find just by playing.
- Maps connect better: no more dead ends, and new houses to visit in town.
- One difficulty for everyone that rises act by act. No difficulty choice and no Ring cap any more.
- Many bug fixes.
- Mine! taps now give a light vibration (only when vibration is on in the settings).
- A nicer intro at the start of the game.
- A natural start: you start out in a bush, play at once, and the story begins when you meet your first wild Spirling.
- Story cutscenes in every act.
- The four regions after the main story each get their own storyline, with duels in Quivermoor.
- A quest book that lists your goals, and "Where next?" shows the way on the map.
- Automatic offline play: without internet the game simply carries on, and your progress syncs once you are back online.
- New bond charms to use with Mine!: single charms for Riftglass, and 3 charm packs in the shop, each with a fixed price and its contents shown before you buy.
- The First Catch Pack at our lowest price: a small pack with fixed contents, shown before you buy. Nothing is random.
- During Mine! you now see the Spirling you are bonding with.
- A 7-day login gift, daily tasks and a Spirling of the day.
- Weekend weather: on Saturday and Sunday a special sky lies over a few areas, and shy Spirlings show up more often there.
- Your Spirlog now shows in percent how many Spirlings you have bonded, with rewards at 25, 50 and 100 % and for Gleam milestones: items and a title.
- Friend codes now work as invites: when a new player joins with your code and plays for a day, you both get a reward.
- Trade with people you meet in the world, and some Spirlings now unfurl through a trade.
- Smoother animations.
- A layout made for iPad.
- Bigger text and bigger buttons that are easier to tap.
- Graphics fixes in every era, from Era I to Era V.
- Security and speed fixes.

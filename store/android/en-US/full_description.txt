Bond with 567 pixel monsters, hunt rare Gleam forms and plan every turn. Chimora is a monster taming RPG and creature collector in retro pixel art, with a dark, funny story that is a little darker than it looks.
• 567 Spirlings to bond: tap the big Mine! button fast to fill the bar
• Tactical turn-based battles with eight elements and Wardens with several phases
• Pixel art that grows up across five eras, from monochrome to full color
• Online game: Chimora needs an internet connection

THE HUSH OF VEYDAL
As a Wayfarer you set out from a lighthouse on the coast of Veydal, choose your first partner and follow Mirela's map book through four regions. Rifts are tearing open everywhere, and only you can seal them again. Everyone else just wants something from you.

TRUST, NOT CAPTURE
Wild Spirlings are never caught. In battle you pick Bond and a big Mine! button appears: tap it as fast as you can. The fuller the bar, the better the bond. In the settings you can switch to the Echo call (repeat the Spirling's call) or to Calm (the echo with no time limit).

TACTICAL BATTLES
• Eight elements, each strong against two and weak against two
• Energy instead of move points, Gifts, Keepsakes and battle fields
• Battles with several Spirlings per side, tag battles and Wardens with several phases
• Damage preview, battle log, auto battle and double speed
• The challenge grows with every act

A STORY WITH TEETH
• Four acts: Veydal, Corvane, Ashmere and the sun-bleached Solenne, with twelve Rifts and their Wardens
• Two clans and eleven strangers, each with a hidden trust value. A Ledger remembers whom you trusted
• Favours can pay off, or end in theft and ambushes. Betrayal is part of the plot, not a bug
• Lore quests for six legendaries and side stories in every act, good for dozens of hours

A WORLD THAT CHANGES

SHOP AND FAIR LEAGUE

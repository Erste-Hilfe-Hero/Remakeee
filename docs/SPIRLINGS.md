## Why each new line has its trait (item 334)

Every trait comes from `BattleRules.TRAITS` (area 2 owns trait effects). One line each:

- **Blotling** slick: an ink blot slides out of any root or vine.
- **Hushkin** steady: born from silence, nothing dazes it.
- **Paddlewisp** last_stand: a whirl spins fastest when the storm is worst.
- **Talusk** thick_hide: a heap of scree shrugs off blows.
- **Fluting** light_sleeper: the wind keeps a reed awake.
- **Glasstarn** slick: a still lake surface gives roots nothing to hold.
- **Lanternel** light_sleeper: a lamp that never goes out never drowses.
- **Seamlet** surge: it sips energy from the Rift edge every turn.
- **Croziel** deep_roots: a fern frond draws on its roots to heal.
- **Brinelet** slick: salt crystals are too smooth to be rooted.

## Waiting for new traits from area 2 (item 335)

None of the new Veydal lines needs a trait that does not exist yet. If area 2 adds traits that fit better
(for example an "ink" or "silence" trait), these lines are the first to switch, and use their current
trait until then: Blotling (slick), Hushkin (steady), Seamlet (surge).

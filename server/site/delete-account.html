Chimora: delete your account and data

In the game (right away): open Chimora, tap Menu → Account → Delete account... and confirm. Your account, all saves, friends, greetings and gifts are removed from the server at once. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Without the game (by email): write to [EMAIL] with the subject "Delete account" and your friend code (shown in the game under Menu → Account, like "ABCD-EFGH"). If you no longer have it, give your Wayfarer name and roughly when you last played. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. We use your email address only for this and delete it afterwards.

What is deleted: player ID, sign-in token and transfer codes, Wayfarer name and saves, friendships, greetings, gifts, world-goal and weekly-board entries. Reports you sent are anonymised. Security logs with IP addresses delete themselves after 7 days.

2. Summary

Chimora is an online game for iOS, Android and Samsung Smart TV. Your save game is stored on our server in the EU (data centre Lauterbourg, France) so you can play and keep your progress. We collect only what the game needs. No ads, no sale of data, no tracking or analytics, no third-party sign-in, no email address required.

On Samsung Smart TV the app works exactly like the phone apps: the same random player ID and save game, nothing more. It does not read TV identifiers (no DUID, no advertising ID / TIFA), viewing data or the Samsung account, and there are no purchases on the TV.

4. Storage on your device

The game stores a copy of your save, your settings and the sign-in token on your device. This is strictly necessary for the service you request (Sec. 25(2) no. 2 German TDDDG) and needs no consent. We do not read advertising identifiers (IDFA / Android Advertising ID).

Notifications on your phone (optional, off by default): if you turn them on in the game and your device allows them, the game plans short notes on your device when a roaming trip is over or a new Find of the Day is waiting (at most one of each kind a day, never between 21:00 and 09:00). They are planned and shown on the device only (local notifications). There is no push service and no device token, and nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off any time under Settings > Game or in your device settings.

5. Recipients

  • Hosting: Contabo GmbH, Munich, Germany (processor, Art. 28 GDPR); server location: data centre Lauterbourg, France (EU). Address: Contabo GmbH, Aschauer Straße 32a, 81549 Munich. Contabo only provides the machine; we run the database on it ourselves.   • Authorities only where required by law.

Apart from this we do not transfer data outside the EU/EEA. The game data itself is stored only in the EU (data centre Lauterbourg, France).

6. Children

Chimora is suitable for all ages. We process only what the game needs, show no ads, do no profiling and offer no free chat (greetings between friends are fixed, translated phrases). Before the shop opens we ask for the birth year and month and keep only the age group: the shop is closed under 13, 13-15 have a fixed limit of 15 EUR a month, 16-17 of 30 EUR. Purchases can also be controlled with the stores' parental controls.

7. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR). Email aa.amuri@icloud.com and include your player ID (shown in the game's settings). Download your data: in the game under Settings → Account → "Download my data" you get everything the server keeps about your account as a JSON file (Art. 15 and 20 GDPR). Other players appear only as "another player". Delete your account: in the game under Settings → Account → "Delete account", or at https://chimora.161-97-102-171.sslip.io/delete-account. Your account and save game are deleted permanently. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the country or German state where you live, or the authority responsible for our seat.

8. Security

App–server traffic is encrypted with TLS. Only the controller has access to the server.

9. Changes

We will update this policy if the game or the law changes and tell you in the game about significant changes.

Chimora is offered through the App Store and Google Play in many countries. We apply the GDPR standard described above to every player, wherever they live. Where the law of your country gives you additional rights, you have those as well. Contact for every request in this section: Ali Amuri Alnasag (the controller from section 1), aa.amuri@icloud.com. What is the same everywhere: your data is stored on our own server, which Contabo GmbH (Munich, Germany) hosts for us in its data centre in Lauterbourg, France (EU). We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, show no ads, do not track you across apps or websites and use no analytics, advertising or crash-report SDKs. From version 1.3.1, after the last game ended unexpectedly, the app sends one short anonymous crash report: only the game version, the device system (for example "Android") and the game scene that was open last. The only evaluation is the anonymous count of game progress from section 3: it is computed from the save games stored with your account (that is why the store privacy labels list game progress as linked to you), but the result contains only numbers per sign-up day, no player ID and no name. Notifications are local only and send nothing (section 4).

4. Storage on your device

The game stores a copy of your save, your settings and the sign-in token on your device. This is strictly necessary for the service you request (Sec. 25(2) no. 2 German TDDDG) and needs no consent. We do not read advertising identifiers (IDFA / Android Advertising ID).

5. Recipients

  • Hosting: Contabo GmbH, Aschauer Straße 32a, 81549 Munich, Germany . The server is in a data centre in Germany. Contabo only provides the machine; we run the database on it ourselves. Contabo processes the data on our behalf under an Art. 28 GDPR agreement. No data is transferred outside the EU.
  • Apple and Google process data as independent controllers when you download the app or buy something.
  • Authorities only where required by law.

Apart from this we do not transfer data outside the EU/EEA. The game data itself is stored only in Germany.

6. Children

Chimora is suitable for all ages. We process only what the game needs, show no ads, do no profiling and offer no free chat (greetings between friends are fixed, translated phrases). Purchases can be controlled with the stores' parental controls.

7. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR). Email aa.amuri@icloud.com and include your player ID (shown in the game's settings). Delete your account: in the game under Settings → Account → "Delete account", or at https://chimora.161-97-102-171.sslip.io/delete-account. Your account and save game are deleted permanently. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the country or German state where you live, or the authority responsible for our seat.

8. Security

App–server traffic is encrypted with TLS. Only the controller has access to the server.

9. Changes

We will update this policy if the game or the law changes and tell you in the game about significant changes.
